# RCG Editorial Evidence Coverage Matrix
Date: 2026-10-02
Status: INTERNAL CONTROL — NOT FOR EXTERNAL DISTRIBUTION

## Purpose
This matrix connects the six current editorial workstreams to the Brand Brain, Claim Registry, Story Vault, and collateral/website authority controls. It is a promotion gate, not marketing copy.

## Promotion rule
No artifact advances from FRAMEWORK/OUTLINE to EVIDENCE CHECKED merely because its reasoning is sound. Each externally usable proposition requires evidence for the exact wording proposed.

## Common proposition-level evidence packet
Every proposition proposed for promotion must record:
1. Claim/proposition ID and exact wording.
2. Source title, owner, source date, retrieval date, and immutable locator where available.
3. Evidence tier: primary contemporaneous / primary retrospective / secondary / marketing-only lead.
4. Classification: FACT / CALCULATION / INFERENCE / OPINION.
5. Reproducible method for calculations or transformations.
6. Contrary, limiting, or disconfirming evidence.
7. Confidentiality and client-information treatment.
8. Relevant Claim Registry IDs.
9. Exact supported wording boundary: what the evidence does and does not permit us to say.
10. Reviewer, status, and review date.
11. Revalidation trigger (new data, methodology change, time expiry, regulatory change, or contradictory evidence).

## Cross-artifact leakage controls
1. **Framework ≠ practice.** A documented framework does not establish that RCG currently operates it.
2. **Example ≠ performance evidence.** A case study cannot establish returns, alpha, hit rate, or representative results.
3. **Research ≠ production.** A researched signal, prototype, or architecture diagram cannot establish live production capability.
4. **Single record ≠ coverage.** One compliant decision record cannot establish firmwide or strategy-wide process adherence.
5. **Source approval ≠ wording approval.** A credible source may support a narrower statement than the proposed sentence.
6. **Artifact approval does not cascade.** Evidence accepted for one article, channel, date, or proposition does not automatically approve another.
7. **Later outcome cannot backfill contemporaneous evidence.** Subsequent success or failure cannot prove what was known, believed, or implemented at the decision timestamp.

## Evidence promotion sequence
Priority for evidence conversion:
1. ED-002 — decision journal: closest to traceable operating records and reusable process evidence.
2. ED-001 — repeatable process: can inherit only independently verified process components from ED-002.
3. ED-005 — infrastructure: factual inventory can be verified component-by-component without investment claims.
4. ED-003 — drawdown control: requires risk methodology plus implementation evidence.
5. ED-004 — cross-asset signals: requires strict point-in-time reproducibility and anti-look-ahead controls.
6. ED-006 — founder philosophy: highest hindsight/cherry-picking risk; promote only after contemporaneous retrieval.

## Release gate
Before any external publication, confirm all of the following:
- every factual/operational proposition has a current evidence packet;
- Claim Registry status permits the exact wording;
- examples were selected under a documented neutral rule;
- performance/AUM/composite/regulatory/assurance claims use their dedicated source of truth and review path;
- confidential positions, clients, counterparties, and non-public operations are excluded or separately approved;
- website/collateral authority is not inferred from stale marketing copy;
- final copy receives compliance review appropriate to its distribution channel.

Failure of any gate returns the artifact to INTERNAL DRAFT.
