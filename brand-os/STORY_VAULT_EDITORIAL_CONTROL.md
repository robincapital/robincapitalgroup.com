# RCG Story Vault & Editorial Control v0.1

Status: INTERNAL DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR EXTERNAL MARKETING  
Parent control: `brand-os/BRAND_BRAIN_EVIDENCE_CONTROL.md`

## 1. Purpose

Turn RCG's research, decisions and operating history into reusable editorial material without converting hindsight, unsupported claims or stale collateral into marketing facts.

## 2. Story record

Each story should retain:
- story ID
- event/date and evidence as-of date
- source links / document IDs
- evidence tier
- contemporaneous facts
- contemporaneous RCG thesis
- uncertainty / competing explanation
- decision or action, if any
- outcome observed later
- counterfactual: what would have invalidated the thesis
- lesson
- approved public angle
- excluded confidential/client-specific material
- controlled claims requiring substantiation
- compliance status
- reusable formats
- last reviewed date

## 3. Narrative sequence

Use this order when a story involves an investment decision:

**FACT → INFERENCE → DECISION → OUTCOME → COUNTERFACTUAL → LESSON**

Never rewrite an inference as a fact because the trade later worked. Never omit a material contemporaneous uncertainty merely because the outcome became obvious in retrospect. Failed or mixed outcomes are eligible stories when the process lesson is useful.

## 4. Editorial pillars

1. **Investment process** — how RCG frames, tests, sizes and revises decisions.
2. **Market structure** — mechanics, liquidity, positioning and cross-asset transmission.
3. **Research in practice** — converting evidence into decisions without false precision.
4. **Capital preservation** — drawdowns, asymmetry, sizing and portfolio construction.
5. **Firm craft** — disciplined research/operating infrastructure for a focused investment firm.
6. **Founder perspective** — lessons and principles tied to documented experience, not mythology.

## 5. Priority score

Score each candidate 0–2 on:
- evidence strength
- distinctiveness to RCG
- usefulness to clients/prospects
- shelf life
- low compliance friction

Maximum = 10. Draft highest-scoring items first. A high score does not waive evidence or compliance review.

## 7. Draft guardrails for current backlog

Until the open evidence exceptions in the Brand Brain are resolved, drafts must not rely on:
- "audited," "verified," "proven" or similar track-record adjectives;
- an assertion that an all-account composite exists or is available;
- unreconciled performance figures;
- stale/superseded investor-deck claims;
- unsupported AUM, client/account counts or superiority claims;
- private client facts, non-public positions or identifying account information.

Performance can be discussed only when the exact source, period, methodology and account/model/proxy/composite status are attached to the draft record.

## 9. Promotion gate

A Story Vault record may move to publication-candidate status only when:
1. evidence links are attached and conflicts resolved;
2. hindsight integrity is checked;
3. confidential/client-specific information is removed;
4. controlled claims map to the Brand Brain claim record;
5. required disclosures are current;
6. compliance review is complete where required.

This document creates an editorial operating system only. It authorizes no external publication.
