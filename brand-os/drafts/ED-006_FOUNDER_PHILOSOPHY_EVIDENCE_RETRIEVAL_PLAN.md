# ED-006 — Founder Philosophy Evidence Retrieval Plan

**Status:** Internal draft / evidence retrieval only  
**Publication status:** BLOCKED pending evidence review  
**Purpose:** Build a defensible Story Vault for “Lessons that actually changed RCG’s investment philosophy” without hindsight reconstruction, cherry-picking, or unsupported firm-practice claims.

## 1. Required evidence chain

A candidate story is not publication-ready unless the record can establish:

`BEFORE STATE → EVENT / EVIDENCE → CONTEMPORANEOUS UPDATE → BEHAVIORAL CHANGE → LATER OUTCOME → CURRENT LESSON`

The later outcome is context, not proof that the earlier decision was correct.

## 2. Two-source minimum

Each promoted story requires, at minimum:

1. **Contemporaneous source:** dated material showing the belief, observation, decision, or update when it occurred.
2. **Persistence source:** separate dated evidence showing that the claimed lesson subsequently changed process, sizing, research, portfolio construction, review, or another observable behavior.

Current marketing copy may be used to locate candidate stories, but may not substantiate its own historical narrative.

## 3. Retrieval buckets

Search existing RCG records for candidates involving:

- thesis formation and falsification;
- sizing and concentration;
- drawdown response;
- liquidity and implementation;
- cross-asset evidence;
- regime identification;
- source quality and data integrity;
- decision review and postmortems;
- automation versus discretionary judgment;
- portfolio-level risk and correlation.

## 4. Candidate record

For every candidate, capture:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Story ID | Stable internal identifier |
| Date range | Earliest relevant contemporaneous date through persistence evidence |
| Before state | What RCG believed or did before the change |
| Trigger | Event, evidence, error, or observation that challenged the before state |
| Contemporaneous source | Exact dated source and location |
| Update | What changed in the thesis/process at the time |
| Behavioral evidence | Observable later change in practice |
| Persistence source | Separate dated source proving the behavior persisted |
| Later outcome | What happened afterward, stated without outcome bias |
| Current lesson | Narrow lesson supported by the evidence |
| Counterevidence | Facts that weaken or complicate the narrative |
| Claim dependencies | Relevant CR/PERF/authority records |
| Confidentiality | Public / anonymize / internal only |
| Review status | candidate / evidence incomplete / rejected / approved for drafting |

## 5. Neutral selection protocol

Do not choose stories because the investment later worked, generated a memorable return, or makes RCG appear prescient.

Before reviewing outcomes:

1. define the retrieval period;
2. define eligible record types;
3. identify all records matching the selected retrieval bucket;
4. record the candidate universe;
5. apply the same evidence test to every candidate;
6. retain rejected candidates and rejection reasons.

If a public piece uses only a subset, document the selection rule.

## 6. Rejection codes

- **R1 — No before state:** original belief/process cannot be established.
- **R2 — Hindsight only:** lesson appears only in later retrospective material.
- **R3 — No behavioral change:** philosophy language changed but observable practice did not.
- **R4 — No persistence evidence:** change cannot be shown beyond an isolated episode.
- **R5 — Outcome-selected:** candidate was selected primarily because the later result was favorable or dramatic.
- **R6 — Claim dependency unresolved:** performance, regulatory, AUM, assurance, or other controlled claim is not cleared.
- **R7 — Confidentiality/provenance failure:** source cannot be used, anonymized, or reliably attributed.

Rejected records remain in the internal candidate log.

## 7. Language controls

Until the evidence chain passes, use research language such as:

- “candidate lesson”
- “records suggest a possible change”
- “requires contemporaneous verification”

Do not use present-tense “RCG does/uses/learned” language merely because a current policy or marketing artifact describes the practice.

## 8. Quarantined claims

This retrieval plan does **not** authorize claims concerning:

- investment performance, alpha, or outperformance;
- AUM, client count, or client characteristics;
- composite construction or proxy-account performance;
- regulatory registration or jurisdictional status;
- “audited,” “verified,” “proven,” or equivalent assurance;
- superiority or best-in-class language;
- confidential client information;
- non-public positions or trading activity.

Any such dependency must route through the applicable Claim Registry, performance source-of-truth, collateral-authority, and compliance review.

## 9. Story Vault promotion gate

A candidate may move from retrieval into editorial drafting only when:

- the evidence chain is complete;
- both minimum source classes are present;
- provenance is recorded;
- counterevidence has been considered;
- the selection method is documented;
- confidentiality is resolved;
- controlled-claim dependencies are cleared or removed;
- the lesson is no broader than the evidence supports.

Passing this gate authorizes **drafting only**, not external publication.

## 10. Recommended retrieval order

Start with records most likely to contain contemporaneous reasoning: investment committee records, decision journals, research notes, portfolio/risk reviews, postmortems, dated model or methodology changes, and archived internal correspondence. Use decks, website copy, and later narratives only as discovery indexes back to primary records.

## 11. Definition of done

ED-006 is ready to become a publishable founder-philosophy piece only after at least one candidate passes the promotion gate and its source packet can withstand a reviewer asking:

> What proves RCG believed this before the outcome, what proves the lesson changed behavior afterward, and what evidence would make the story less flattering?

Until then, ED-006 remains an internal evidence-retrieval program.
