# RCG Controlled Claim Registry

Status: INTERNAL CONTROL — NOT PUBLICATION-READY  
Purpose: Convert recurring brand, authority, performance, personnel, regulatory, and firm-history statements into auditable claim records before they enter external distribution.

## Status vocabulary

- **GREEN** — primary evidence identified and wording bounded to that evidence.
- **YELLOW** — plausible but evidence, scope, period, or wording is incomplete.
- **RED** — conflicting evidence, unsupported representation, or material ambiguity. Do not publish.
- **EXPIRED** — evidence may once have supported the claim but is stale for current use.

A GREEN status does not itself constitute compliance approval. External investment marketing still requires the applicable review process.

## Claim record minimum fields

Every claim promoted beyond idea stage should carry:

1. Claim ID and exact proposed wording.
2. Claim class.
3. Evidence source(s), owner, and retrieval date.
4. Applicable entity, strategy/account universe, and period.
5. Calculation methodology where numerical.
6. FACT / CALCULATION / INFERENCE / OPINION tag.
7. Known limitations and contradictory evidence.
8. Status and reviewer.
9. Approved wording boundary.
10. Expiry/revalidation trigger.

## Revalidation triggers

Re-open a claim whenever any of the following occurs: new ADV/regulatory filing; new performance period or methodology; strategy rename; personnel/role change; address change; new public collateral; conflicting source discovered; evidence older than the intended "current" representation; or a material change in the facts underlying the wording.

## Drafting rule

Editorial work may reference a claim ID while it is YELLOW or RED for internal analysis, but public-facing copy must not silently convert unresolved claims into facts. Where the story is valuable without the controlled claim, remove the claim rather than weaken its disclosure through vague wording.
