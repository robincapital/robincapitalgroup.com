## Editorial evidence packet standard

Every draft gets a companion packet containing:
1. source/document identifier and evidence tier;
2. source date and evidence as-of date;
3. exact factual proposition supported;
4. calculations with inputs and reproducible method, if any;
5. fact/inference/opinion classification;
6. material counterargument or uncertainty;
7. controlled-claim flags;
8. confidential exclusions;
9. disclosure requirement;
10. reviewer/status and last-reviewed date.

A source can support more than one proposition, but each proposition must be mapped explicitly. A draft does not inherit substantiation merely because another RCG document makes the same claim.

## Production states

`IDEA → EVIDENCE GATHERING → INTERNAL OUTLINE → INTERNAL DRAFT → EVIDENCE CHECKED → COMPLIANCE REVIEW → PUBLICATION CANDIDATE`

No item may skip **EVIDENCE CHECKED**. Items involving performance, specific securities/positions, testimonials/endorsements, rankings/awards, AUM/client counts, regulatory status, or comparative/superiority language require explicit controlled-claim review before promotion.

## Current exclusions
